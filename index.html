    HOW TO PLAY

    Matching dice score their sum × a multiplier that grows with rarity. Pair × 2, two pairs × 3, three of a kind × 4, straight and full house × 5 (all five dice), four of a kind × 6, five of a kind × 8. Three 3s = 9 × 4 = 36; four 4s = 16 × 6 = 96.
