    HOW TO PLAY

    Match values, not big numbers. A pair of 1s is worth the same as a pair of 6s.

    Save a connected set of four for your second hand. The first hand must leave four dice you can still snake through.

    1. Eight dice and one gold ×2 cell on a 3×3 board.
    2. Swipe four dice that touch edge to edge (no diagonals, no repeats) to bank a hand. Both hands together use all eight dice.
    3. Tap a die to hold it. REROLL changes every unheld, unbanked die. Two rerolls per level, shared by both hands.
    4. Tap the gold cell to arm ×2 for the hand you bank next. If it is still unused on the last hand, it is applied there automatically.
    5. Reach the target with the two hands to clear the level.
